Research Program

Characterizing and optimizing the immunomodulatory potential of first-line chemotherapeutics to eliminate resistance, prevent lethal recurrence, and enable durable downstream chemoimmunotherapy benefit.

Primary Focus

Characterizing & Optimizing Chemoimmunomodulation

Chemotherapy is not purely cytotoxic—first-line agents uniquely remodel antigen presentation, immunogenic cell death, proteostasis, and immune cell infiltration. Our primary focus is dissecting and optimizing these immunomodulatory effects to maximize chemotherapy efficacy, immunologic benefit, and response durability, which in turn drives downstream immunotherapy synergy.

Computational & AI

Multi-Omic & Predictive Frameworks

Using bulk, single-cell, and spatial transcriptomics alongside machine learning pipelines, we build predictive classifiers that capture divergent chemoimmunomodulatory induction trajectories across patient tumors and preclinical models.

Downstream Translation

Overcoming Resistance & Enhancing Chemoimmunotherapy

By understanding what governs chemoimmunomodulatory heterogeneity in aggressive cancers—such as triple-negative breast cancer and brain metastases—we identify rational regimens that prevent lethal recurrence and improve downstream checkpoint blockade outcomes.

Culture & Approach

Integrated Bench, Computation & Mentorship

Every project pairs computational discovery with molecular and cellular biology validation, while training the next generation of scientists through mentorship, innovation, and a culture of excellence.
