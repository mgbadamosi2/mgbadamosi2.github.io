Research Program

Characterizing and optimizing the immunomodulatory potential of first-line chemotherapeutics to eliminate resistance, prevent lethal recurrence, and enable durable downstream chemoimmunotherapy benefit.

01 · Primary Focus

Chemoimmunomodulation

Chemotherapy is not purely cytotoxic: first-line agents uniquely remodel antigen presentation, immunogenic cell death, senescence, and immune cell infiltration. We integrate molecular and cellular biology with computational and mathematical models to characterize and predict chemoimmunomodulatory induction trajectories.

02 · Translational Impact

Chemotherapy & Immunotherapy Synergy

By optimizing how chemotherapeutics remodel the tumor microenvironment, we aim to enhance chemotherapy efficacy, immunologic benefit, and response durability, unlocking stronger downstream synergy with immune checkpoint blockade and cellular therapies.

03 · Aggressive Disease

Breast to Brain Metastasis

Brain metastases remain a major driver of lethal recurrence in triple-negative breast cancer. We investigate the unique immune and metabolic landscape of breast-to-brain metastases to uncover actionable vulnerabilities and overcome therapy resistance.

Culture & Approach

Integrated Bench, Computation & Mentorship

Every project pairs computational discovery with molecular and cellular biology validation, while training the next generation of scientists through mentorship, innovation, and a culture of excellence.
