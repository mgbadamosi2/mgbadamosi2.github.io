Research Program

Understanding how chemotherapy shapes anti-tumor immunity to design smarter, patient-tailored combination immunotherapies.

Focus Area 1

Chemoimmunomodulation in Triple-Negative Breast Cancer

Chemotherapy is not purely cytotoxic—different agents uniquely remodel antigen presentation, immunogenic cell death, proteostasis, and immune cell infiltration. We study how these immunomodulatory effects govern synergy or resistance with anti-PD-1 checkpoint blockade in TNBC and brain metastases.

Focus Area 2

Computational & AI-Driven Precision Immuno-Oncology

Using bulk, single-cell, and spatial transcriptomics alongside machine learning pipelines, we develop predictive classifiers that capture divergent chemoimmunomodulatory induction trajectories across patient tumors and preclinical models.

Focus Area 3

Translational Pharmacogenomics & Disparities

Building on our work in pharmacogenomics and targeted oncology, we investigate host and tumor-intrinsic determinants of drug response to ensure emerging chemoimmunotherapies work equitably across diverse patient populations.

Approach

Wet-Lab Meets Dry-Lab

Every project pairs experimental pharmacology and immune profiling with quantitative modeling—creating a tight feedback loop between bench discoveries and patient-relevant biomarkers.
