The Gospel

God's Love and Redemption

As you read through this page, I hope to share the joy and wonder I have found in God's love and redemption, and perhaps encourage you to explore that love for yourself.

Have you ever been shown kindness you did not earn or deserve? In a small way, that captures what grace and salvation mean. Yet why do we need salvation in the first place? Because every one of us has fallen short of God's standard.

"For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God."
(Romans 3:23)

Salvation came to us purely out of God's love, even when we were far from Him. God, our loving Father, longs for a real relationship with us. This was His purpose in creating us. We are so precious to Him that He gave His very best to bring us into His family. That love was shown through the death and resurrection of Jesus Christ, who bore the consequences of our wrongs on our behalf.

"But God demonstrates His own love toward us, in that while we were still sinners, Christ died for us."
(Romans 5:8)

Through this incredible act of love, we are welcomed into God's family and made new.

This relationship with God is open to anyone. Just as He reached out to me, God is reaching out to you. Love is only complete when it is received, and by putting your faith in Jesus and accepting His gift of grace, you step into a life of hope and peace. If you ever want to talk about faith, life, or science, my door is always open.
